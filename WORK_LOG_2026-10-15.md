# Work Log: INT8 TensorRT Engine - 2026-10-15

## Objective
Build an INT8 TensorRT engine using Kaggle pothole dataset images for calibration, targeting ≥60 FPS on Jetson Orin Nano.

## Key Findings
- INT8 engine achieves **167.8 FPS** in pure trtexec benchmark (well above 60 FPS target)
- Background frame reader improves wall-clock FPS from 54→101 FPS on demo.mp4
- Video encoding (mp4v) reduces FPS by ~60% — should be disabled for speed benchmarks
- INT8 detects fewer potholes than FP16 (1553 vs 2483) but runs 2.8x faster
- INT8 engine is 2.3x smaller (13 MB vs 30 MB)
